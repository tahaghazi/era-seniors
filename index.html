Students who understand the class. Makers who can build it.

We sit where you sit. We know the committee votes, the budget arguments, the group chat at 2am, and the deadline that is always sooner than it looks.

THE FACTORY SIDE

Our partners own the production line. That means honest lead times, controlled fabric quality, and a price that has no middleman folded into it.

07 — HOW IT WORKS

With you from the first idea, at every step.

Seven stages, and every one of them is ours. Because the design desk and the production line sit inside the same business, your order never gets handed to a stranger halfway through.

  1. 01
    التخطيط
    PLANNING

    Committee meeting: the pieces, the head-count, the budget and the date it all has to land.

  2. 02
    التصميم
    DESIGN

    Crest, year and colourway drawn on the real silhouette until the committee signs it off.

  3. 03
    القماش
    FABRIC

    Chosen by weight and hand-feel, then sourced before a single stitch is cut.

  4. 04
    التنفيذ
    PRODUCTION

    Cut, sewn and embroidered on our own line — no outsourcing, no surprises.

  5. 05
    التدقيق
    QUALITY CHECK

    Every piece inspected against the approved sample before it leaves the floor.

  6. 06
    التغليف
    PACKAGING

    Bagged and labelled by student name and size, so handover day takes minutes.

  7. 07
    النقل
    DELIVERY

    One drop to campus, with spare sizes held back for exchanges.
